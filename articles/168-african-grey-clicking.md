# What Does It Mean When an African Grey Clicks? Reading Grey Parrot Sounds

African grey parrots are famous for their vocal skills: words, whistles, phone rings, microwave beeps. Among all those sounds, many owners notice **clicking**. Tongue clicks, beak clicks, rapid clicking bursts.

**Clicking is a common African grey sound.** What it means depends on context and body language.

## Common reasons African greys click

**1. Natural vocalization**
Greys have a large range of natural sounds, and clicks are part of it. Wild greys use a wide variety of calls and sounds.

**2. Contentment**
Many owners notice soft clicking when their grey is relaxed, especially while settling down, preening, or sitting with them.

**3. Mimicry**
Greys copy sounds from their environment. Clicking can come from:
- **Your tongue clicks**
- **Keyboard or mouse clicks**
- **Door latches, pens, light switches**
- **Clicker training** tools

**4. Excitement or attention-seeking**
Rapid clicking can accompany excitement, like when you come home or a favorite treat appears.

**5. Warning (with body language)**
In some cases, clicking combined with **pinning eyes** (pupils rapidly shrinking), **raised feathers on the neck**, a **low, forward posture**, or **growling** can be a warning to back off.

## Read the body language

| Clicking plus... | Likely meaning |
|---|---|
| Relaxed feathers, calm posture | Contentment |
| Copying a household sound | Mimicry |
| Bouncing, flapping, head bobbing | Excitement |
| Pinning eyes, raised neck feathers, lunging | Warning, give space |

## When sounds might signal a problem

Clicking noises that come from **breathing** are different from vocal clicks. See an avian vet if you notice:
- Clicking or wheezing **with each breath**
- **Tail bobbing** while breathing
- **Open-mouth breathing** at rest
- **Nasal discharge**, sneezing
- **Fluffed feathers**, lethargy, or appetite changes

## FAQ

**Why does my African grey make clicking noises?**
Clicking is a common vocalization. It can mean contentment, excitement, or mimicry of household sounds.

**Is clicking a sign of aggression in parrots?**
Sometimes, when combined with pinning eyes, raised feathers and a lunging posture.

**Can African greys mimic clicking sounds?**
Yes, they often copy clicks from people, devices and objects around the home.

**When is clicking a health concern?**
If it's tied to breathing, with tail bobbing, open-mouth breathing or other signs of illness.
