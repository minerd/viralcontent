# Why Are My Geese Digging Holes? Foraging, Mud Baths and Nesting

Geese are known for grazing, not digging. So when your yard starts filling up with holes, muddy pits and bare patches, it can be surprising.

There are a few common reasons geese dig, and most are completely natural.

## 1. Foraging for food

Geese use their bills to probe and pull at soil, especially when it's soft after rain. They're after:
- **Roots and tender shoots**
- **Worms, grubs and insects**
- **Seeds**

Over time, repeated foraging in the same spot turns into a hole.

## 2. Mud around water

Geese love water. Where they drink or splash, soil gets wet, and geese dabble and dig in it, turning it into muddy pits. Around a pool or water trough, this can happen fast.

**What to do:**
- Place water containers on **gravel, pavers, or a raised platform**
- **Move water sources** regularly so one spot doesn't get destroyed
- Provide a proper pool area with drainage

## 3. Grit

Geese swallow small stones and coarse particles to help grind food in their gizzard. Some digging is searching for grit.

**What to do:** offer **insoluble poultry grit**.

## 4. Nesting

In spring, a female goose may scrape out a shallow nest in the ground and line it with grass, straw and her own down.

**Signs:** a shallow, rounded scrape, often near cover, with the goose spending more time there.

**What to do:** provide nesting material and a sheltered nest area if you plan to hatch eggs, or collect eggs daily if not.

## 5. Boredom

Geese confined to a small space with little to do may dig more.

**What to do:** rotate them to fresh pasture, provide greens, and give them space to roam safely.

## Protecting your yard

- **Rotate grazing areas** to let grass recover
- **Fence off** gardens and new seedlings
- **Fill holes** to prevent ankle injuries (for people and birds)
- Keep water on **hard or gravel surfaces**

## FAQ

**Why do my geese dig in the dirt?**
Mostly to forage for roots, bugs and grit, and around water they dabble and make mud.

**Do geese dig nests?**
Females often scrape a shallow nest in the ground in spring.

**How do I stop geese from making mud holes?**
Put water on gravel or pavers, move water sources often, and rotate grazing areas.

**Do geese need grit?**
Yes. Provide insoluble poultry grit if they don't have access to natural small stones.
