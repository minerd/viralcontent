# iOS 27: Where Did My Photo Albums Go? Finding and Pinning Them Again

Search this and you get two useless things: threads about **iOS 18**, and data-recovery products insisting your photos are deleted.

**Your albums are almost certainly fine.** Since Apple's Photos redesign, albums are no longer a tab at the bottom — they're a **section inside one long scrolling view**, and most people simply can't find them.

## Get to your albums right now

**Fastest:** open Photos and **scroll down**. Below the main grid you'll find the collections area, with **Albums** among the sections.

**Faster, once you know it:** on the Photos screen, **press and hold the "Albums" section heading** — that brings up a list of all your albums at once, instead of scrolling the carousel.

**Also useful:** scroll to the very bottom and tap **Customize & Reorder**.

## Fix the layout so it stops happening

**Customize & Reorder** is the setting that makes the app usable again:

1. Photos → scroll to the bottom → **Customize & Reorder**
2. **Tick** the sections you want visible (make sure **Albums** is ticked)
3. **Untick** sections you never use — Memories, Featured Photos, Wallpaper Suggestions, Media Types you don't care about
4. **Drag** Albums to the top so it's the first thing under the grid
5. Done

You can also **pin** individual albums so they sit at the top of the Albums section. Open an album, use the **…** menu, and pin it. Do that for the three or four you actually use and you'll never hunt again.

## If a specific album really is missing

Work through these:

- **Hidden album turned off.** Settings → Apps → Photos → **Show Hidden Album**. If it's off, the Hidden album doesn't appear anywhere — that's by design
- **Recently Deleted.** Also under Settings → Apps → Photos (**Show Recently Deleted Album**). Deleted items stay there about 30 days
- **Shared albums.** If shared albums vanished, check Settings → Apps → Photos → **Shared Albums** is on, and that you're signed into iCloud with the right Apple Account
- **Wrong Apple Account.** Settings → top of the list: confirm it's your usual account, especially after a restore or a new phone
- **iCloud Photos still syncing.** After setting up a new device, albums populate as the library downloads. Leave it on Wi-Fi and power overnight
- **A smart/auto album you're thinking of** (People, Places, Media Types) may be unticked in Customize & Reorder rather than missing

## What not to do

- **Don't buy photo-recovery software** because an album heading moved. Those pages rank for this search and have nothing to do with the redesign
- **Don't sign out of iCloud** to "refresh" — with iCloud Photos on, that can start a lengthy re-download and panic you further
- **Don't delete and reinstall** anything; Photos is a system app and your library isn't stored in it

## FAQ

**Did Apple remove albums?**
No. They moved from a bottom tab into a section of the single Photos view, and can be reordered or pinned.

**How do I see all albums in a list?**
Press and hold the Albums section heading, or tap into the section and use the list view.

**Can I get the old tab layout back?**
Not exactly, but Customize & Reorder plus pinned albums gets you within a tap of the old behaviour.

**Why are my shared albums gone?**
Check Shared Albums is enabled in Photos settings and that you're on the correct Apple Account.
