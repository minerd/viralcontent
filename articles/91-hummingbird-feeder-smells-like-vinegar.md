# Why Does My Hummingbird Feeder Smell Like Vinegar? (Your Nectar Fermented)

If you search this question, you'll mostly find articles about cleaning hummingbird feeders *with* vinegar. That's not your problem. Your problem is that the **nectar itself** smells sour, like vinegar or cheap wine.

**Short answer: the nectar has fermented.** It needs to be dumped and the feeder cleaned.

## Why nectar turns into vinegar

Hummingbird nectar is just sugar water, which is also a perfect food for **wild yeasts and bacteria**. They get in from the air, from insects, and from the birds' own beaks.

1. **Yeast** eats the sugar and makes alcohol (and bubbles).
2. **Bacteria** can then turn that alcohol into **acetic acid**, which is vinegar.

Heat speeds up every step. In hot weather, nectar can start to spoil within a day or two.

## Signs your nectar has gone bad

- **Sour, vinegar or alcohol smell**
- **Cloudy** nectar (fresh nectar is clear)
- **Bubbles** or foam
- **Stringy bits** or film
- **Black or dark spots** (mold) in the feeder or ports
- Hummingbirds visiting less than usual

## Is fermented nectar harmful?

Spoiled nectar isn't good for hummingbirds, and moldy feeders are a concern. That's why bird organizations and experienced feeders stress changing nectar often and keeping feeders clean. When in doubt, dump it.

## How often to change nectar

A commonly used rule of thumb: **the hotter it is, the more often you change it.**

| Daytime temperature | Change nectar about every… |
|---|---|
| Very hot (90°F+ / 32°C+) | 1 day |
| Warm (80s °F / high 20s °C) | 2 days |
| Mild (70s °F / 20s °C) | 3–4 days |
| Cool (60s °F and below) | up to about a week |

Change it sooner if you see any signs of spoiling.

## How to clean a sour feeder

1. **Dump the nectar.** Don't top it off.
2. **Take the feeder apart** completely.
3. **Scrub** with hot water and a bottle brush. Use small brushes or pipe cleaners for the ports.
4. For mold or buildup, **soak in a vinegar-water solution** (roughly 1 part vinegar to 4 parts water is common), or a very dilute bleach solution for heavy mold. Then **rinse thoroughly**.
5. **Dry completely** before refilling.

## Prevent it next time

- **Make smaller batches** so nectar doesn't sit around. Only fill feeders partway in hot weather.
- **Use the standard recipe:** about **1 part white sugar to 4 parts water**. No honey, no brown sugar, no red dye.
- **Store extra nectar in the fridge** and use it within about a week.
- **Hang feeders in shade.** Sun speeds fermentation.
- **Choose feeders that are easy to take apart and clean.**

## FAQ

**Why does my hummingbird nectar smell like vinegar?**
It has fermented. Yeast turns the sugar into alcohol, and bacteria can turn that into vinegar.

**Is fermented nectar bad for hummingbirds?**
Spoiled nectar isn't good for them. Dump it and clean the feeder.

**How often should I change hummingbird nectar in hot weather?**
In very hot weather, about every day. In cooler weather, every few days.

**Why is my hummingbird nectar cloudy?**
Cloudiness is a sign of spoilage. Replace it and clean the feeder.
