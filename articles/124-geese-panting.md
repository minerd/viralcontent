# Is It Normal for Geese to Pant? Heat, Stress, and Breathing Problems

If you keep geese, you've probably seen them standing around with their bills open, breathing quickly. Is that normal?

**Often, yes.** Like other birds, geese don't sweat, so panting is one of their main ways to cool down. But panting in cool weather, or with other symptoms, can mean something's wrong.

*General information. Contact a vet familiar with waterfowl if your goose seems unwell.*

## Normal reasons geese pant

**1. Heat**
In warm weather, geese may:
- Pant with **open bills**
- **Hold their wings slightly away** from their bodies
- Seek **shade and water**
- Rest more

**2. Exertion**
After running, chasing, flapping or a bout of honking at the neighbor's dog, some panting is normal and should settle within minutes.

**3. Stress or excitement**
Being herded, handled, or startled can cause temporary panting.

## Heat stress warning signs

Take action if a goose:
- Is **panting heavily and can't settle**, even in shade
- Seems **weak, wobbly or lethargic**
- **Won't get up**
- Is **not drinking**

**How to help:**
- Provide **deep shade**
- Offer **plenty of cool, fresh water**, including water deep enough to dunk their heads (geese, like ducks, dunk to cool off and keep their nostrils clean)
- A **kiddie pool** in the shade is a favorite
- Improve airflow in shelters
- For a struggling goose, contact a vet

## When panting may mean illness

Be concerned if a goose is panting or breathing with an open bill **when it isn't hot or active**, especially with:
- **Wheezing, gurgling or rattling**
- **Discharge** from the nostrils or eyes
- **Swollen eyes or face**
- **Tail bobbing** with each breath
- **Weakness**, not eating, isolation from the flock

Waterfowl can develop respiratory infections, including fungal infections linked to moldy bedding or feed. These need veterinary attention.

## Prevention tips

- **Clean, dry bedding**; remove anything damp or moldy promptly
- **Fresh feed** stored dry
- **Ventilated shelters**
- **Shade** and **water** available at all times in warm months

## FAQ

**Why is my goose breathing with its mouth open?**
Usually to cool down in heat or after activity. Open-mouth breathing in cool weather can be a sign of illness.

**How do geese stay cool?**
They pant, hold their wings out, seek shade and dunk in water.

**Do geese need a pool?**
They need water deep enough to dunk their heads. A shallow pool is a great way to help them stay cool and clean.

**What are signs of respiratory illness in geese?**
Wheezing, discharge, swollen eyes, tail bobbing, weakness and poor appetite.
