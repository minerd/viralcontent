# Why Are My Bantam Chicks Dying? 8 Causes and How to Protect the Rest

Bantam chicks are adorable and **tiny**, sometimes about half the size of standard chicks. That small size makes them more vulnerable to problems that bigger chicks survive.

Losing chicks is heartbreaking, and when several die, you need to find the cause fast. Here's a checklist.

*General information. If multiple chicks are dying, contact a vet or your local poultry extension service, especially for testing.*

## 1. Chilling

Bantams lose heat fast.
**Signs:** chicks **huddled under the heat source**, loud distressed peeping.
**Fix:** check brooder temperature at chick level; provide a warm zone and a cooler zone.

## 2. Overheating

**Signs:** chicks **spread out far from the heat**, panting, wings held out, lethargic.
**Fix:** raise the heat source or reduce heat; make sure chicks can move away from it.

## 3. Drowning or getting wet

Tiny chicks can fall into waterers, get soaked and chilled, or drown.
**Fix:** use **chick-sized waterers**, and add **marbles or clean pebbles** to the water tray so chicks can drink without falling in.

## 4. Not finding food or water

Some chicks don't figure out eating and drinking quickly.
**Fix:** gently **dip each chick's beak** in water when they arrive. Make sure feed is easy to reach. For very small bantams, some keepers grind crumbles finer.

## 5. Pasty butt

Droppings stick to the vent and can block it.
**Signs:** crusted poop over the vent.
**Fix:** gently clean with warm water, check several times a day, and review brooder temperature (stress and temperature swings can contribute).

## 6. Coccidiosis

A common parasitic disease in young chicks, often linked to damp, dirty bedding.
**Signs:** lethargy, hunched posture, fluffed feathers, **bloody or watery droppings**, sudden deaths.
**Fix:** contact a vet quickly. Keep bedding dry and clean. Many keepers use medicated starter feed or vaccinated chicks, following guidance.

## 7. Trampling and crushing

Bantams housed with larger chicks can get trampled, especially around heat sources.
**Fix:** brood bantams separately or with similarly sized chicks, and provide enough space.

## 8. Weak hatch or shipping stress

Some chicks are weak from the start, from incubation problems or long shipping.
**Fix:** provide warmth, water and food immediately on arrival; some losses in the first days can happen even with good care.

## When several chicks die

- **Isolate sick chicks**
- **Clean and dry the brooder**
- Keep notes on symptoms, ages and timing
- **Contact a vet or extension service**, especially if deaths continue; some poultry diseases are reportable

## FAQ

**Why do my bantam chicks keep dying?**
Common causes include chilling, overheating, drowning, not eating or drinking, pasty butt, coccidiosis and trampling.

**What temperature do bantam chicks need?**
Chicks need a warm zone under the heat source and a cooler area. Watch their behavior: huddling means too cold, spreading out means too hot.

**Can bantam chicks drown in waterers?**
Yes. Add marbles or pebbles to the water tray.

**What does coccidiosis look like in chicks?**
Lethargy, fluffed feathers, hunched posture, and bloody or watery droppings.
