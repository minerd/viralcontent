# Why Is My Box Turtle Staying in the Water? Soaking, Overheating or Illness

Box turtles are land turtles, not aquatic ones. So when yours spends hours sitting in its water dish, it's natural to wonder why.

Some soaking is completely normal. Constant soaking can be a clue that something in the enclosure, or the turtle, needs attention.

*General care information. See a reptile vet if your box turtle shows any signs of illness.*

## Normal: soaking to hydrate

Box turtles **drink and absorb moisture** by soaking. Many soak regularly, especially:
- After eating
- In warm weather
- Before or after a period of hiding

**Normal if:** your turtle soaks for a while, then gets out, eats, explores and burrows as usual.

## Reason 1: The enclosure is too hot

A turtle that's too warm may sit in water to cool down.

**Check:** measure temperatures with a digital thermometer at the basking spot and the cool side. Box turtles need a **gradient**: a warm area and a clearly cooler area. Make sure there's shade and hiding spots.

## Reason 2: The enclosure is too dry

Box turtles generally need **moderate to high humidity** and moist substrate they can burrow into. In a dry enclosure, they may head for the only damp spot: the water dish.

**Check:** use a hygrometer. Offer **moist, burrowable substrate** (like a soil/coconut fiber mix with leaf litter) and mist as needed for your species.

## Reason 3: Shedding or skin issues

Soaking can help loosen skin and scutes. But constant soaking with **flaky, discolored skin or shell** may point to skin or shell problems.

## Reason 4: Parasites or irritation

Mites and skin irritations can lead to more soaking.

## Reason 5: Illness

Be concerned if soaking comes with:
- **Swollen or closed eyes** (common in box turtles with vitamin A deficiency or infections)
- **Bubbles or discharge** from the nose or mouth, or noisy breathing
- **Not eating**
- **Lethargy**
- **Soft shell** or abnormal shell growth
- **Lopsided swimming** or floating

**Action:** see a reptile vet.

## Water safety for box turtles

Box turtles are **not strong swimmers**. Deep water can be dangerous.
- Water dish should be **shallow**: roughly up to the turtle's chin or the bottom edge of its shell when standing
- Wide enough to climb **fully in and out easily**
- **Cleaned and refilled daily**, because they often poop in water

## FAQ

**Why does my box turtle sit in its water dish all day?**
It may be too hot or too dry, or it might be shedding, irritated or unwell. Check temperatures and humidity, and watch for illness signs.

**Is it normal for box turtles to soak?**
Yes. Regular soaking helps them stay hydrated.

**Can box turtles drown?**
They aren't strong swimmers, so water should be shallow and easy to climb out of.

**Why are my box turtle's eyes swollen?**
Swollen eyes can be linked to infections or vitamin A deficiency. See a reptile vet.
