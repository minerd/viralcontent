# Why Is My Hamster Squeaking When Sleeping? Dreams, Comfort Noises, or Breathing Trouble

You lean in close to your sleeping hamster and hear it: tiny squeaks, maybe a twitch or two. Cute? Worrying? Both?

**Most of the time, occasional soft squeaks during sleep are normal.** But there's one type of sleep noise you shouldn't ignore: squeaky or clicking *breathing*. Here's the difference.

*General information only. Respiratory problems in hamsters can become serious quickly, so see a vet if you're unsure.*

## Normal reasons for sleep squeaks

**Dreaming.** Many mammals twitch, move their paws and make small sounds during deep sleep. Hamsters seem to be no exception. Short, occasional squeaks with twitching are usually just that.

**Settling in.** Hamsters often make little noises while shifting around in their nest, rearranging bedding or getting comfortable.

**Being disturbed.** Hamsters are mostly active at night and sleep during the day. If you're checking on them in daylight, a noise, light or vibration can wake them with a grumpy squeak.

**Dreams in young hamsters.** Young hamsters are often more twitchy and vocal in sleep.

## Noises that are NOT normal

Listen carefully to *how* the sound happens.

**Clicking, wheezing or squeaking with each breath** is different from occasional squeaks. It often points to a **respiratory infection**, which is one of the more common serious illnesses in pet hamsters.

Other signs to watch for:
- Sneezing
- Runny nose or crusty eyes
- Breathing with the mouth open or visible effort
- Lethargy, hunching, puffed-up fur
- Eating less, weight loss

If breathing sounds are present, **see an exotic-pet vet promptly**. Respiratory infections in small animals can get worse fast.

## Check the environment

Some breathing irritation comes from the cage setup:
- **Dusty bedding** or scented bedding can irritate airways. Choose dust-extracted, unscented paper bedding.
- **Cedar or pine shavings** are commonly advised against for small rodents because of aromatic oils.
- **Ammonia from dirty bedding.** Spot-clean the pee corner regularly.
- **Drafts or cold rooms.** Keep the cage away from windows, doors and AC vents.
- **Strong smells** like air fresheners, candles and cleaning sprays near the cage.

## A quick home check

1. **Record a video** of the sleep sounds. It's much easier to show a vet than describe.
2. **Watch the chest.** Is the sound regular with every breath, or random?
3. **Check the nose and eyes** once your hamster is awake.
4. **Weigh** your hamster and keep a note.
5. **Note activity at night.** Is your hamster still running, eating and exploring normally?

## The short version

| Sound | Likely meaning |
|---|---|
| Occasional soft squeak + twitching | Dreaming, normal |
| Squeak when woken up | Annoyed, normal |
| Squeak/click/wheeze with every breath | Possible respiratory issue, see a vet |
| Sleep noises + sneezing, discharge, lethargy | See a vet promptly |

## FAQ

**Do hamsters make noises when they sleep?**
Yes, small squeaks and twitches during sleep are common and usually normal.

**Why is my hamster clicking when breathing?**
Clicking or wheezing with each breath can signal a respiratory infection. See a vet.

**Do hamsters dream?**
Many mammals show dream-like sleep behavior such as twitching and small sounds, and hamsters often do too.

**What bedding is best to avoid breathing problems?**
Dust-extracted, unscented paper-based bedding is commonly recommended. Avoid scented bedding and aromatic wood shavings.
