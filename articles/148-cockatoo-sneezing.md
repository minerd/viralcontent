# Is It Normal for Cockatoos to Sneeze? Powder Down, Dry Air and When to Worry

If you search this question, most results talk about **cockatiels**, the small cousins of cockatoos. But cockatoos have their own reasons for sneezing, and one of them is right there on your furniture: dust.

**Occasional sneezing is normal for cockatoos.** Frequent, wet or messy sneezing is not.

*General information. See an avian vet if your cockatoo shows any signs of illness.*

## Why cockatoos sneeze

**1. Powder down**
Cockatoos are **powder down birds**. Special feathers break down into a fine powder that helps waterproof and condition their plumage. That powder is also why cockatoo owners find white dust everywhere. A little of it gets into the nostrils, and a sneeze clears it.

**2. Dry air**
Indoor heating in winter dries the air. Dry nasal passages can lead to sneezing.

**3. Bathing and drinking**
Water up the nose after a bath or a vigorous drink can cause a sneeze or two.

**4. Irritants**
Birds have very sensitive airways. Sneezing can be triggered by:
- **Aerosol sprays**, air fresheners, perfume
- **Scented candles** and plug-ins
- **Smoke** of any kind
- **Dusty bedding** or seed dust

## Normal vs worrying sneezing

**Usually normal:**
- **Occasional**, dry sneezes
- Clean, dry nostrils (nares)
- Bird is active, eating, vocal, and playful

**See an avian vet if you notice:**
- **Frequent sneezing**
- **Wet sneezes** or **discharge** from the nares
- **Crusty, blocked or stained** feathers above the nostrils
- **Swollen eyes** or discharge
- **Tail bobbing**, open-mouth breathing, clicking or wheezing
- **Fluffed up**, sleepy, eating less
- **Changes in voice** or droppings

Birds hide illness well, so don't wait for multiple symptoms.

## How to reduce sneezing at home

- **Bathe or mist your cockatoo regularly** (many love showers), which helps manage powder down
- **Run an air purifier** with a HEPA filter in the bird room
- **Use a humidifier** in dry winter months (keep it clean)
- **Avoid aerosols, scented products and smoke**
- **Clean the cage often** and wipe dust from surfaces
- **Keep birds out of the kitchen**: fumes from overheated non-stick cookware can be deadly to birds

## FAQ

**Why is my cockatoo sneezing?**
Occasional sneezing is often from powder down, dry air, water or irritants. Frequent or wet sneezing may signal illness.

**Are cockatoos dusty birds?**
Yes. Cockatoos are powder down birds and produce a lot of feather dust.

**What does a respiratory infection look like in a cockatoo?**
Nasal discharge, wet sneezes, swollen eyes, tail bobbing, open-mouth breathing and lethargy.

**Does bathing help a sneezy cockatoo?**
Regular bathing or misting can help manage powder down and dry air.
