# Siri AI Not Working in iOS 27? 4 Common Problems and What Actually Helps

The new **Siri AI** is the biggest change to Siri in years. It has its own app, understands what's on your screen, remembers context, and can take actions across apps.

It's also still labeled **beta**, and it shows. Apple's support forums have filled up with the same handful of complaints since iOS 27 launched on September 14.

Here are the four problems people run into most, and what has helped. Fixes below are reported by users on Apple's forums, not official Apple guidance.

## Problem 1: "Sorry, something's gone wrong. Please try again."

**What happens:** Siri AI answers small talk fine, but fails on anything substantial with a generic error. One Apple Community thread has dozens of "me too" replies from iPhone 16 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro and even MacBook Air owners.

**What people tried that didn't help:** restarting, resetting network settings, changing region, turning off VPNs and ad blockers, even reinstalling iOS 27.

**What one user suggests:** force Siri to re-download its files by switching voices.
1. Go to **Settings > Siri > Siri Voice**.
2. Pick a different dialect or voice.
3. Wait for it to fully download.
4. Switch back to your preferred voice.

It hasn't been widely confirmed, but it's quick and harmless. If it doesn't work, submit feedback to Apple. Apple doesn't actively reply on its user forums, so the official feedback channel is the way to be heard.

## Problem 2: Siri AI won't do simple things offline

**What happens:** "Add a reminder for 6 p.m. tomorrow" works offline on an older iPhone with classic Siri. But on devices with Siri AI, the same request offline fails with "you'll need to turn off Airplane mode" or "I'm having trouble with the connection."

That's a real step backwards if you rely on Siri on planes, in the subway or in places with bad signal.

**What helps:** the user who reported it said switching back to classic Siri brought offline functionality back: "The functionality has returned and is useable again."

**Workaround if you keep Siri AI:** for offline reminders and timers, use the apps directly or set up **Shortcuts** for your most common requests.

## Problem 3: Timers and alarms broken on Apple Watch

**What happens:** Siri on Apple Watch says "the Clock app is not on the device" when you ask for a timer.

**What helps:** turn Siri off and on in iPhone Settings, then restart both devices. If that fails, turning off Siri AI (Beta) has fixed it for several users. We have a [full guide to this one](/watchos-27-siri-timer-not-working-fix).

## Problem 4: Siri AI isn't showing up at all

Common reasons:
- **Your device isn't supported.** Siri AI needs Apple Intelligence, which requires an iPhone 15 Pro or newer.
- **You're still on the waitlist.** Users report the wait ranging from a few hours to a few days. Keeping your phone plugged in and on Wi-Fi overnight seems to help it finish setting up.
- **Your iPhone is a China-market model.** More on that in our guide [why you can't get Siri AI](/why-cant-i-get-siri-ai).
- **Language.** At launch, Siri AI works in English only.

## Should you switch back to classic Siri?

Honestly, for a lot of people, yes, for now.

**Switch back if:** you mostly use Siri for timers, reminders, alarms, music and smart home, especially offline. Classic Siri is less clever but more dependable at those.

**Keep Siri AI if:** you like asking longer questions, want it to understand what's on your screen, or enjoy trying new features and don't mind bugs.

## Keep an eye on iOS 27.0.1

Apple has said an iOS 27.0.1 update is coming soon, mainly for an iPhone 18 Pro Face ID bug. Point updates often include other fixes too, so install updates as they arrive and retest.

## FAQ

**Why does Siri AI say "Sorry, something's gone wrong"?**
It appears to be a bug in the Siri AI beta. Users report trying a voice switch to force a re-download of Siri files; otherwise, submit feedback to Apple and wait for an update.

**Does Siri AI work offline?**
Users report that basic requests like reminders can fail offline with Siri AI, while classic Siri handles them.

**Can I go back to the old Siri in iOS 27?**
Yes. Users report switching from Siri AI (Beta) back to standard Siri in Settings.

**Which iPhones support Siri AI?**
iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, meaning iPhone 15 Pro and newer.
