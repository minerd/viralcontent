# Zella: A Free Mac Screen Recorder With No Watermark That Edits for You (Offline)

Anyone who's searched "free screen recorder for Mac" knows the pattern. The free version stamps a giant watermark on your video, or caps you at 720p, or wants you to create an account and upload everything to its cloud.

**Zella**, which launched on Product Hunt in mid-September, takes a different approach. The free version has **no watermark, no account, no time limit**, and everything, including the AI editing, **runs on your own computer**. It even works in airplane mode.

Here's what it does and where the catch is.

## What Zella does

Zella is a screen and camera recorder for **Mac and iPhone** with built-in AI editing. After you record, it can:

- **Cut silences and filler words** automatically
- **Write captions** on-device, in 19 languages
- **Clean up your voice**
- **Add music**
- **Remove or replace your background**
- **Reframe** for 9:16 (Shorts, Reels, TikTok), 1:1 or 16:9
- **Color grade** with 43 looks on Mac (42 on iPhone)

Its one-tap "Auto-Polish" does several of these at once. There's also an AI assistant with 101 actions on Mac (71 on iPhone).

## The privacy angle

This is Zella's real differentiator. According to the company: "Recording, captions, background removal, voice cleanup and export all run on your own hardware... The app works in airplane mode."

That matters if you're recording anything confidential: internal demos, client work, unreleased products, or anything with personal info on screen. Nothing gets uploaded to edit it.

## Free vs Pro

**Free:**
- Up to **1080p**
- **No watermark**
- No account needed
- Exports up to **2 minutes** long
- 7 days of Pro features on every new install

**Pro: $89, one time**
- One purchase covers both Mac and iPhone
- No subscription

## The catch

The **2-minute export limit** on the free version. That's fine for quick demos, bug reports, social clips and short tutorials. It's not fine for a 20-minute course lesson.

If you mostly make short videos, the free version may be all you need. If you record longer content, you're looking at the $89 Pro license. For a one-time price, that compares well to subscription screen recorders, but it's worth knowing up front.

## Requirements

- **Mac:** macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple Silicon or Intel
- **iPhone:** iOS 18 or later

## Who it's for

- **Creators making short-form video** who want captions and silence cutting without learning an editor
- **Product people and support teams** recording quick demos and bug reports
- **Teachers** making short explainer clips
- **Anyone recording sensitive screens** who doesn't want cloud uploads

## Who should look elsewhere

- **Windows users.** It's Mac and iPhone only.
- **Long-form recorders on a zero budget.** The 2-minute free limit will get in your way. macOS's built-in screen recording (Shift + Command + 5) is free and unlimited, just without the AI editing.

## FAQ

**Is Zella free?**
Yes. The free version records up to 1080p with no watermark and no account, with exports up to 2 minutes. Pro is a one-time $89.

**Does Zella add a watermark?**
No, not even on the free version.

**Does Zella work offline?**
Yes. Recording, captions, background removal, voice cleanup and export all run on-device, and the app works in airplane mode.

No. It's available for Mac (macOS 14+) and iPhone (iOS 18+).
