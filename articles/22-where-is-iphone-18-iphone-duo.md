# Where's the Regular iPhone 18? Apple's Weird 2026 Lineup, Explained

If you walked into an Apple Store this month looking for a normal iPhone 18, you probably left confused.

There's an iPhone 18 Pro. There's an iPhone 18 Pro Max. There's a folding iPhone that costs as much as a used car. But the plain, regular iPhone 18? Not here.

That's not a mistake. Apple split its lineup this year, and if you're planning to upgrade, it changes what makes sense to buy.

## What Apple launched this fall

At its September 9 event, Apple announced:

- **iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max.** New A20 Pro chip, camera upgrades, and higher starting prices than last year's Pro models. On sale since September 18.
- **iPhone Duo.** Apple's first foldable iPhone, with a book-style design that opens into a larger inner screen. Apple calls it the thinnest iPhone ever when open.
- **AirPods 5, Apple Watch Series 12 and Watch Ultra 4.**

## What's missing (and when it's coming)

According to coverage of the event, the regular **iPhone 18**, the **iPhone Air 2** and the budget **iPhone 18e** aren't part of the fall launch. They're expected in **spring 2027**.

So Apple has effectively created two launch seasons: premium phones in the fall, everyday phones in the spring.

## So what should you actually do?

Here's my honest take, depending on who you are.

**You have an iPhone 15 or older and want a normal-priced phone.**
Wait for spring. The regular iPhone 18 is the phone most people buy, and it's months away. If your current phone is dying, last year's iPhone 17 is the sensible buy right now, and it may get cheaper.

**You always buy the Pro.**
Nothing's changed for you. The iPhone 18 Pro is out. Just be aware of the early Face ID restart bug that Apple says it's fixing with a software update.

**You're curious about the foldable.**
It's a first-generation product at $1,999 and up. First versions of new designs tend to be the most expensive and the most likely to have quirks. If you love new tech and the price doesn't hurt, go for it. If you're buying it to keep for four years, wait for reviews and maybe the second generation.

**You're on a budget.**
The iPhone 18e isn't expected until spring either. Don't pay full price for an old budget model now without checking for discounts.

## Why would Apple do this?

Apple hasn't laid out every reason, but the effect is clear: Pro and foldable buyers get the spotlight in the fall with no cheaper model pulling attention away, and Apple gets a second big sales moment in spring instead of one giant one.

For you, it just means one thing: **don't assume "iPhone 18" means one phone anymore.**

## FAQ

**Is there a regular iPhone 18?**
Not yet. It's expected in spring 2027, along with the iPhone Air 2 and iPhone 18e.

**How much is the iPhone Duo?**
It starts at $1,999 for 256GB and goes up to $3,199 for 2TB.

**When can I buy the iPhone Duo?**
Pre-orders open October 16, 2026, with release on October 23 in the first wave of countries.

**Should I buy the iPhone 17 now or wait for the iPhone 18?**
If you need a phone now, the iPhone 17 is a reasonable choice. If your current phone works fine, waiting until spring gets you the new model.
