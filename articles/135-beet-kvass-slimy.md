# Why Is My Beet Kvass Slimy? What Causes It and Whether to Keep It

Beet kvass is one of the simplest ferments: beets, salt, water, time. It should end up as a deep ruby, salty-earthy, slightly tangy drink.

So when you pour it and it comes out **thick, stringy or slimy**, like syrup or egg white, it's off-putting. Here's what's happening.

## What makes kvass slimy

Slimy ferments are often described as **"ropy."** Ropiness is usually caused by certain types of bacteria that produce thick, stringy compounds as they ferment sugars. Beets are naturally sweet, which gives them plenty to work with.

It's more likely when:
- The ferment is **warm**
- The ferment is **young**, before the microbes balance out
- There's **not much salt**
- Equipment or ingredients introduced unwanted microbes

The same phenomenon shows up in kombucha, water kefir, tepache, cider and wine.

## Is slimy kvass safe?

Slime alone doesn't always mean the kvass is dangerous, and sometimes ropiness passes. But:

**Throw it out if you see or smell:**
- **Fuzzy mold** on the surface or beets
- **Pink, black or colorful growth**
- A **rotten, putrid or unpleasant** smell rather than earthy-sour
- Soft, mushy, **slimy beets** that smell bad

**When in doubt, throw it out.**

## If it smells fine and there's no mold

Some fermenters:
1. Leave it a **few more days** to see if the ropiness breaks down
2. **Stir gently**
3. Move it somewhere **cooler**

If it's still slimy and unappealing, start a fresh batch.

## How to prevent slimy beet kvass

- **Ferment at cool room temperature**, out of direct sun and away from heat
- **Use enough salt** according to a trusted recipe
- **Keep beets submerged** under the brine (use a weight)
- **Chop beets into cubes**, not grated (grated beets release more sugar quickly and ferment very fast, and some recipes warn they can produce more alcohol and off flavors)
- **Use clean jars and utensils**
- Some people add a splash of **brine from a previous healthy batch** or other active ferment as a starter

## What should beet kvass taste like?

Salty, earthy, slightly sour, with a clean, bright flavor. If it's fizzy, sour and pleasant, it's working. If it's sweet, flat and slimy, it may need more time or a fresh start.

## FAQ

**Why is my beet kvass thick and slimy?**
Usually a "ropy" phase caused by certain bacteria producing thick compounds, often when the ferment is warm.

**Is slimy beet kvass safe to drink?**
Discard it if there's mold, colorful growth or a bad smell. If unsure, throw it out.

**Will the slime in kvass go away?**
Sometimes ropiness breaks down with a few more days of fermentation.

**Should I grate beets for kvass?**
Most recipes recommend cubes rather than grated beets, which can ferment too fast.
