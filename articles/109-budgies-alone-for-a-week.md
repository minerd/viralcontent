# Can You Leave Budgies Alone for a Week? Why Not, and What to Do Instead

You've got a trip coming up. Your budgies are small, they don't need walks, and you've seen automatic feeders online. Could they manage on their own for a week?

**No, a week alone is too long for budgies.** Even with extra food and water set out, too much can go wrong. Here's why, and what to do instead.

## Why a week alone is risky

**1. Food problems you won't see**
Budgies often eat seed from the top of the bowl and leave empty husks. A bowl can **look full of seed but actually be husks**, and a budgie can go hungry next to it.

**2. Water gets dirty or runs out**
Birds dunk food and poop in water. Within a day or two, water can be contaminated. Bottles can jam or leak.

**3. Equipment fails**
Automatic feeders and waterers can clog, jam, or run out. If something fails on day two, your budgies have nothing for five days.

**4. Budgies hide illness**
Small birds can get sick quickly and often hide symptoms until they're seriously unwell. A daily check catches problems early.

**5. Accidents**
A toe caught in a toy, a fight between cage mates, a night fright that injures a wing, a cage door that pops open.

**6. They're social birds**
Budgies are flock animals and get bored and stressed without interaction, especially single budgies.

## How long can budgies be left alone?

For most healthy adult budgies, many owners are comfortable with **no more than about a day** with plenty of fresh food and water set out. Anything longer needs **someone checking in daily**.

## Better options

**1. A pet sitter visiting daily**
The best option for many birds, since they stay in their familiar cage. The sitter should:
- **Blow off husks** and refill seed daily
- **Change water** daily
- Offer fresh veggies if that's part of the routine
- Check the birds are active and eating
- Know your **avian vet's contact details**

**2. A friend or neighbor**
Same checklist as a pet sitter. Leave clear written instructions.

**3. Bird boarding**
Some avian vets, bird shops and specialist boarders take birds. Ask about disease precautions and cage separation.

**4. Take them with you**
For some road trips, a travel cage works. Consider stress, temperature and your destination.

## Before you leave

- Leave **written care instructions** and your vet's number
- **Show the sitter** how to check for husks versus seed
- Stock **extra food**
- Keep the room at a steady temperature, away from drafts and direct sun
- Keep the usual **light schedule** (a timer on a lamp helps)

## FAQ

**How long can budgies be left alone?**
Generally no more than about a day, with plenty of food and water. Longer absences need daily check-ins.

**Can budgies survive a week without people?**
It's too risky. Food, water, equipment and health problems can go unnoticed.

**Are automatic feeders safe for budgies?**
They can help, but they can jam or run out, so they shouldn't replace daily checks.

**What does a bird sitter need to do?**
Refresh seed (removing husks), change water, check the birds daily, and know how to contact your avian vet.
