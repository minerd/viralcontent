# Why Does My Russian Tortoise Hiss at Me? (It's Not Really Hissing)

You go to pick up your Russian tortoise, and it pulls into its shell with a sharp **hiss**. It feels like a warning. Is your tortoise angry at you?

**Usually not.** That hiss is mostly a side effect of pulling in quickly.

## Why tortoises "hiss"

A tortoise's lungs sit right under its shell. When it pulls its head and legs in **fast**, there's suddenly less space inside, and **air is pushed out of its lungs**. That rush of air makes the hissing sound.

So the hiss usually means:
- Your tortoise was **startled**
- It **felt threatened** by something approaching from above (like a hand)
- It was **surprised** by being lifted

It's a reflex, not a planned threat.

## How to reduce the hissing

- **Approach from the front or side**, where your tortoise can see you, rather than from above
- **Move slowly** and give it a moment to notice you
- **Lift gently** with your hand supporting the underside (plastron) and keep the tortoise level
- **Keep handling short**. Many tortoises tolerate handling but don't love it
- **Build positive associations**: offer food by hand, spend time near the enclosure

Many tortoises hiss less over time as they get used to their keepers.

## When sounds mean illness

Hissing when retracting is normal. But watch for sounds that happen **while breathing normally**, not while pulling in:
- **Wheezing, clicking, whistling or gurgling**
- **Bubbles** from the nose or mouth
- **Open-mouth breathing**
- **Nasal discharge**
- **Puffy eyes**
- **Lethargy** and **reduced appetite**

These can be signs of a **respiratory infection**, which needs veterinary care. Respiratory issues in tortoises are often linked to husbandry problems like **low temperatures**, drafts, or poor humidity.

## Husbandry check

For Russian tortoises, make sure you have:
- A **warm basking area** and a cooler side
- **UVB lighting** appropriate for tortoises
- **Burrowable substrate** and hides
- **Fresh water** in a shallow dish
- Protection from **drafts** and cold floors

## FAQ

**Why does my tortoise hiss when I pick it up?**
Air is pushed out of its lungs when it pulls into its shell quickly. It's usually a startle response.

**Is my tortoise angry when it hisses?**
Usually not. It's more of a reflex from being startled.

**Should I worry if my tortoise makes noise when breathing?**
Yes, wheezing, clicking or bubbling during normal breathing can signal a respiratory infection. See a vet.

**How do I get my tortoise used to me?**
Approach slowly from the front, handle briefly and gently, and offer food by hand.
