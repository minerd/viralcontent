# How to Scan Several Pages Into One PDF With ChatGPT

Search "scan multiple pages into one PDF" and you get Adobe, Smallpdf and printer software — all written years before this feature existed.

ChatGPT's iOS app now has a **camera scan flow that combines several captured pages into one PDF**. If you've been photographing documents one at a time and then asking it to make sense of them, this replaces that.

## How to use it

1. Open the **ChatGPT app on iOS** (make sure it's updated)
2. Tap the **camera / attach** control in the composer
3. Choose the **scan** option rather than a plain photo
4. **Capture each page in turn** — the app detects edges and keeps adding pages to the same document
5. Review the page order, retake any that didn't come out
6. **Finish** — the pages are combined into a **single PDF** you can send into the conversation, save, or share out

Because the result is one file rather than eight separate images, everything downstream gets better: you can ask for a summary of the whole document, have it pull specific figures across pages, translate it, or extract a table without the model losing track of page order.

## What it's genuinely good for

- **Multi-page letters, contracts and statements** you want summarised or explained
- **Handouts and notes** you want turned into a study sheet
- **Receipts and invoices** to total up or itemise
- **Forms** you want explained before you fill them in
- Anything where the next step is a question, not just storage

## What it isn't

- **Not an OCR archive tool.** If your goal is a searchable, text-layer PDF for long-term filing, dedicated scanner apps and desktop OCR still do that better
- **Not a fax or a signature flow**
- **Not private by default.** You are uploading the document to a service. Don't scan other people's medical records, ID documents, or anything under a confidentiality obligation into a chat assistant without checking what you're allowed to do. Check your account's data controls if that matters to you
- **Not a replacement for a flatbed** when image quality matters (photos, artwork, fine print)

## Getting better scans

- **Flat, even light**, no hard shadow across the page — a window plus a lamp beats a flash
- **Plain contrasting background** so edge detection works
- **Phone parallel to the page**, not angled
- **One page per capture**, and don't let the facing page creep into frame on a bound book
- For bound books, press the spine flat or photograph pages separately
- Check the **order** before you finish; reordering afterwards is more work

## When Notes is still the better tool

Apple's **Notes** app (Scan Documents) and **Files** have done multi-page PDF scanning for years, offline, with no upload, and with a reliable text layer. If you just want the PDF and nothing else, use those — then attach the finished file to ChatGPT if you want it analysed.

The ChatGPT flow wins when **scanning and asking are the same task**.

## FAQ

**Does this work on Android?**
The multi-page scan flow rolled out on iOS first. Check your app version; feature availability moves quickly and by region.

**Can I get the PDF out of the chat?**
Yes — the scanned document is a file you can save or share from the conversation.

**How many pages can it combine?**
It's designed for ordinary documents, not books. Practical limits come from upload size and how much the model can usefully read at once.

**Is the text searchable?**
Treat it as an image-based PDF unless you've confirmed otherwise. For a reliable text layer, use a dedicated scanner app.
