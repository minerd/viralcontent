# Is It Normal for Lovebirds to Sneeze? Dust, Water and When to Call the Vet

Lovebirds are small, active and chatty, and yes, they sneeze. The question is how often, and what else is going on.

**An occasional dry sneeze is normal.** Frequent sneezing or sneezing with other symptoms is a reason to see an avian vet.

*General information. Birds hide illness well; don't wait for multiple symptoms before calling a vet.*

## Normal reasons lovebirds sneeze

- **Clearing dust** or bits of food from their nostrils (nares)
- **Water up the nose** after bathing or drinking
- **Dry air**, especially in winter with heating on
- **Seed dust** from their food

## Irritants that trigger sneezing

Birds have very sensitive respiratory systems. Common irritants include:
- **Aerosol sprays**, air fresheners, perfume, hairspray
- **Scented candles** and plug-in fresheners
- **Smoke** from cooking, cigarettes, fireplaces
- **Dusty bedding** or cage liners
- **Cleaning products** used near the cage

**Important:** fumes from **overheated non-stick cookware** can be deadly to birds. Keep birds far from the kitchen.

## When sneezing is a warning sign

See an avian vet if you notice:
- **Frequent sneezing** or sneezing fits
- **Wet sneezes** or **discharge** from the nares
- **Crusty or stained feathers** above the beak
- **Swollen, watery or crusty eyes**
- **Tail bobbing**, open-mouth breathing, clicking or wheezing
- **Fluffed feathers**, sleepiness, eating less
- **Voice changes**
- If you have **multiple birds** and more than one is sneezing

## Reducing sneezing at home

- **Air purifier** with a HEPA filter in the bird room
- **Humidifier** during dry months (kept clean)
- **Regular baths** or misting
- **Clean food and water dishes** daily
- **Low-dust cage liners** (plain paper works well)
- **No aerosols, scents or smoke** near birds

## FAQ

**Why is my lovebird sneezing?**
Occasional sneezing clears dust or water. Frequent or wet sneezing may signal illness.

**Is sneezing a sign of illness in lovebirds?**
It can be, especially with discharge, tail bobbing, fluffed feathers or appetite changes.

**Can dry air make my bird sneeze?**
Yes. A humidifier and regular baths can help.

**What fumes are dangerous to birds?**
Overheated non-stick cookware, aerosols, smoke and strong cleaning products are common hazards.
