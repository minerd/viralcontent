# Spotify Taste Profile: How to Fix Your Recommendations (Kids' Music, Sleep Sounds and All)

If you share Spotify with a kid, you know the problem. You play "Baby Shark" on a road trip once, and for the next three months your Discover Weekly thinks you're four years old.

Spotify's new **Taste Profile** is the fix a lot of people have wanted for years. It shows you how Spotify interprets your taste, and lets you change it by just typing what you want.

It rolled out in the US on September 24, 2026. Here's how to use it.

## What Taste Profile is

Taste Profile shows you how Spotify reads your preferences across **music, podcasts and audiobooks**. Then, instead of fiddling with thumbs-down buttons one song at a time, you can type requests in plain language, like "less sleep sounds" or "more variety," and Spotify adjusts your recommendations.

Spotify first showed it off at SXSW in March 2026, tested it in New Zealand, and has now brought it to the US.

## Who can use it

Right now:
- **United States** only
- **Premium** subscribers only
- **18 and older**
- Still in **beta**, with no timeline for a wider release

## How to find it

1. Open Spotify and tap your **profile picture**.
2. Scroll to **Taste Profile**.
3. Look at how Spotify describes your taste.
4. Under **"Tell us more,"** type your request.

Changes "should appear after a few hours," according to Spotify, so don't expect your Home screen to transform instantly.

If you don't see it, check that you're on Premium, update the app, and remember it's a beta that may not have reached every account yet.

## 15 requests worth typing

### Cleaning up shared-account mess
1. "Ignore the kids' music. That's my daughter, not me."
2. "Stop recommending sleep sounds and white noise."
3. "The meditation podcasts were a one-time thing."
4. "My partner uses this account for workout music. Keep that out of my recommendations."

### Getting out of a rut
5. "More variety. I keep hearing the same 20 artists."
6. "Introduce me to newer artists similar to what I like, not the famous ones."
7. "More music from outside the US and UK."
8. "I like '90s hip-hop but I'm tired of the biggest hits."

### Setting the mood
9. "More calm, instrumental music for focus."
10. "Less sad music overall, please."
11. "More upbeat stuff in the mornings." [ADD: note whether time-based requests work once tested]

### Podcasts and audiobooks
12. "More long-form interview podcasts, fewer news shows."
13. "Recommend audiobooks in history and science, not self-help."

### Fixing mistakes
14. "I listened to one Christmas album in July. That was a joke."
15. "I don't actually like country music, I was at a party."

## Tips for better results

- **Be specific.** "Less pop" is vague. "Less mainstream chart pop from the last few years" is clearer.
- **Say why if it helps.** "That was my kid" helps Spotify understand it should ignore a whole category, not just one song.
- **Give it a few hours** before judging results.
- **Adjust in small steps.** Change one thing at a time so you can see what worked.

## What about outside the US?

Spotify hasn't given a date for other countries. If you're not in the US or New Zealand, the classic tools still help: use **"Exclude from your Taste Profile"** on playlists (like sleep or kids' playlists), and mark things "Not interested."
