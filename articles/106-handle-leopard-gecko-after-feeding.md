# Can You Handle a Leopard Gecko After Feeding? How Long to Wait and Why

Your leopard gecko just finished a big meal of crickets or mealworms, and you want some cuddle time. Should you pick them up?

**Best practice: give them time to digest first.** Many experienced keepers wait **at least a day (often 24–48 hours)** after a full meal before handling.

*General care information. If your gecko regurgitates food repeatedly or seems unwell, see a reptile vet.*

## Why waiting matters

**1. Regurgitation**
Handling, squeezing or stressing a gecko with a full stomach can cause it to **regurgitate** its meal. That's not just messy; repeated regurgitation can be a sign of stress or health problems and means your gecko isn't getting its nutrition.

**2. Digestion needs warmth**
Leopard geckos rely on external heat to digest. After eating, they usually go to the **warm side** of the enclosure to let digestion happen. Taking them out onto a cooler hand or room interrupts that.

**3. Stress**
Being picked up can feel like a predator grab to a small reptile. Adding stress on top of digestion is best avoided.

## How to plan handling around feeding

A simple routine many keepers use:

- **Feeding day:** feed, then leave them alone to bask and digest
- **Day after:** leave them alone, or only minimal interaction
- **Handling days:** on days between feedings, when they're not digesting a meal

For adults fed every few days, this naturally leaves handling days in between. Juveniles eat more often, so handling should be lighter and shorter anyway.

## Other times to avoid handling

- **Right before or during a shed**, when skin is sensitive
- **When they're new** to your home. Give them a week or two to settle before regular handling
- **When they seem stressed**: tail waving, hissing, vocalizing, running away
- **When they're unwell**

## Handling tips

- **Scoop from below**, don't grab from above
- **Keep sessions short**, especially at first
- **Sit on the floor** or over a soft surface in case they jump
- **Never grab the tail.** Leopard geckos can drop their tails when stressed
- **Wash your hands** before and after (reptiles can carry salmonella)

## FAQ

**How long should I wait to hold my leopard gecko after eating?**
Many keepers wait around 24–48 hours after a full meal to let the gecko digest.

**Can handling make a leopard gecko throw up?**
Yes. Handling too soon after eating can cause regurgitation.

**Can I handle my leopard gecko every day?**
Short, gentle sessions are fine for many geckos, but avoid handling right after meals and during sheds.

**Why is my leopard gecko regurgitating?**
Common causes include handling after feeding, low temperatures, oversized prey, or illness. See a vet if it happens more than once.
