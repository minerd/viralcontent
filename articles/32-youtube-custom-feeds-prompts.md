# YouTube Will Let You Build Your Own Algorithm. 25 Custom Feed Prompts to Try

For years, the YouTube algorithm has been something that happens *to* you. Watch one video about a weird kitchen gadget and you're getting kitchen gadgets for a week.

That's about to change. At its Made on YouTube event on September 23, 2026, YouTube announced **custom feeds**: you describe the kind of videos you want in your own words, and YouTube uses Gemini AI to build a feed around that description. Each feed shows up as its own tab pinned to your YouTube homepage.

It doesn't replace your normal recommendations. It sits next to them. You switch between feeds when you want to.

## When you can use it

YouTube says support for creating multiple custom feeds will start rolling out **next month** (October 2026) on **web and mobile**. Rollouts are usually gradual, so it may take a few weeks to reach your account.

## How it works

1. Open the custom feed option on YouTube. [ADD: exact button location once live]
2. Type what you want the feed to show, in normal language.
3. You can say what to **include**, what to **exclude**, and what to **prioritize**.
4. The feed appears as a tab on your homepage.
5. Make as many as you like.

YouTube's own examples are telling: "video podcasts to watch during your 30-minute train commute" or "relaxing commentary videos to unwind with." In other words, you can build feeds around a *moment* or a *mood*, not just a topic.

## What makes a good feed prompt

From how AI prompts work in general, a few habits will likely help:

- **Say what you don't want.** "No reaction videos" or "no clickbait thumbnails" probably does as much work as what you do want.
- **Set a length.** "Under 15 minutes" or "long-form, over 30 minutes" changes everything.
- **Describe the vibe.** "Calm," "funny," "no yelling," "expert-level."
- **Name the situation.** Commute, dinner, falling asleep, learning.

## A few honest caveats

- **It's still an algorithm.** Gemini is interpreting your words, and it'll get things wrong sometimes. Expect to tweak your prompts.
- **Your main feed doesn't change.** If you want your default homepage to improve, you'll still need to use "Not interested" and watch history controls.
- **It works for kids' feeds only if the account allows it.** Check YouTube's family and supervised account settings before relying on it.

## FAQ

**What are YouTube custom feeds?**
Feeds you create by describing, in your own words, what videos you want. YouTube uses Gemini to build the feed, and it appears as a tab on your homepage.

**When are YouTube custom feeds available?**
YouTube announced on September 23, 2026 that the feature will begin rolling out on web and mobile the following month.

**Does it replace the YouTube algorithm?**
No. Custom feeds sit alongside your normal recommendations. You can switch between them.

**Can I make more than one custom feed?**
Yes. YouTube says you can create multiple custom feeds.
