# ToneBird: The AI That Drafts Replies in Your Voice (Without Sending Anything)

Most AI writing tools have the same problem: everything they write sounds like the same polite robot. Your message to your boss, your best friend and your landlord all come out in identical "I hope this message finds you well" English.

**ToneBird**, which launched on Product Hunt on September 23, tries to fix that by remembering *who* you're talking to. It drafts your next reply "in your voice," adjusted to the relationship, across the apps you already use.

## What ToneBird does

ToneBird is a desktop app for **Mac and Windows** that drafts replies inside:

Gmail, Slack, WhatsApp, iMessage, Google Chat, Discord, WeChat, Lark, X, Reddit, LinkedIn and Instagram.

Its main features:
- **Drafts replies in your voice**, learning from how you write
- **Remembers relationships** and conversation context with each person
- **Tone buttons:** More Precise, Warmer, Add Humor
- **Calendar integration** for scheduling meetings in replies
- **Tracks commitments** you make in conversations ("I'll send that Friday")

## The design choice that matters most

**ToneBird does not send messages.** It only puts a draft in the reply box. You read it, change it if you want, and hit send yourself.

That's the right call. The worst-case scenario for an AI reply tool isn't a slightly awkward draft; it's an awkward message *sent* to your boss at 11 p.m. Keeping the human on the send button avoids a whole category of disasters.

It also helps you avoid becoming a "meat proxy," the new slang for someone who forwards AI text without reading it. If you have to look at it before sending, you'll at least read it.

## Privacy

According to ToneBird: "Your tone profile, person cards, learned corrections, and usage counts live on your device. Export or delete them any time in Settings."

That's reassuring for the *profile*. But drafting replies means the AI has to read the messages you're replying to. Before using it on work accounts, check:
- Whether message content is sent to a cloud AI model to generate drafts
- Your employer's rules about third-party tools reading work messages

## Who it's for

- People who **reply to a lot of messages across many apps**: freelancers, founders, managers, community moderators
- People who find replying **draining**, not difficult: you know what to say, you just don't want to type it 60 times a day
- **Non-native English speakers** who want replies that sound natural but still like themselves

## Who probably doesn't need it

- If you mostly reply to a few close people, you don't need a relationship database.
- If you're on your phone most of the time: it's desktop-only right now.

## FAQ

**What is ToneBird?**
A Mac and Windows app that drafts replies in your voice across Gmail, Slack, WhatsApp, iMessage and other apps, remembering context for each contact.

**Is ToneBird free?**
There's a free plan with 15 daily uses and 30 contacts. Paid plans start at $9.90 a month.

**Does ToneBird send messages automatically?**
No. It only inserts drafts into the reply box for you to review and send.

**Does ToneBird work on iPhone?**
Not currently. It's available for macOS and Windows.
