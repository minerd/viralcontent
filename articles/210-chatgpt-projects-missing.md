# ChatGPT Projects Missing? Where Your Chats and Projects Went

You open ChatGPT and your **Projects are gone**, or the sidebar looks different and your pinned chats have vanished. Dozens of threads in OpenAI's own community forum say the same thing every time the app changes.

**In almost every reported case the data still exists.** Work through these in order before assuming anything was deleted.

## 1. Check the web app

This is the single most common resolution. The **desktop app and the web app don't always have the same feature set**, and migrations between app versions have imported **chats but not projects**.

Go to **chatgpt.com in a browser**, signed into the same account. If your projects are there, nothing is lost — it's a client problem, not a data problem.

## 2. Look for a collapsed section

Sidebar redesigns move things into collapsible groups. Check for:

- A **Projects** section that's collapsed to a single row
- A **Pinned** section (pinned chats moved into their own group and it can arrive collapsed)
- A **"See more" / chevron** at the end of a short list
- The sidebar being in a narrow or hidden state entirely

Expand everything once and the panic usually ends there.

## 3. Confirm which account you're in

The second most common cause. If you have a personal account and a work/Enterprise or Team account, or you signed in with Google on one and email on the other, you're looking at a different workspace with a different history.

Check the account/workspace switcher at the bottom of the sidebar. Projects do not move between workspaces.

## 4. Search instead of scrolling

You don't need to find a chat in a list. Use **Search chats** and type any phrase you remember from the conversation — it matches across your whole history, including conversations inside projects. This is also the fastest way to prove a chat still exists.

## 5. Give a migration time

After a major account or app change, OpenAI has told users that **migration can take time** and projects reappear afterwards. If everything else checks out, wait a few hours before doing anything drastic.

## 6. Clear the client, carefully

If the web app shows your projects and the installed app doesn't:

- **Sign out and back in** in the app
- **Update** the app (an old build can predate a feature)
- In a browser: a **hard reload**, then clearing **cookies and site data for chatgpt.com only** — some users recovered a broken sidebar this way. You'll have to sign in again, so don't do this if you rely on a session you can't re-create

## What actually does delete things

- **You deleted the project or chat.** Deletions are permanent once processed — there's no recycle bin to restore from
- **Temporary chats.** Conversations started in a temporary/incognito mode are not saved to history by design
- **An account change** — a workspace you were removed from takes its content with it
- **Retention policy** on a Team/Enterprise workspace, set by your admin

## Keep it from mattering next time

- **Export your data**: Settings → Data controls → Export data. You get an archive of your conversations by email
- Don't treat a chat as the only copy of anything you care about — paste important outputs into your own notes or repo
- For project instructions and files you rely on, keep a copy outside the app

## FAQ

**Did OpenAI delete my projects?**
Almost never. The usual causes are a client that didn't import them, a collapsed sidebar section, or the wrong account.

**Why does the desktop app show chats but no projects?**
Migrations have imported conversations without projects; the web app has been the reliable place to reach them.

**Can I recover a project I deleted myself?**
No. Deletion is permanent — which is why an export is worth doing while things are fine.

**My chats are all in one flat list now. Is that a bug?**
That's a layout change people have reported after updates, not data loss. Search still finds everything.
