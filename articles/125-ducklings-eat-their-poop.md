# Is It Normal for Ducklings to Eat Their Poop? Why It Happens and How to Discourage It

You look into the brooder and see it: a duckling happily nibbling at… poop. Gross, yes. Unusual? Not really.

**Ducklings peck at almost everything**, and droppings are part of that. It's fairly common, but it's worth keeping to a minimum, because a dirty brooder can make ducklings sick.

## Why ducklings eat poop

**1. They peck at everything**
Ducklings explore with their bills. Anything on the ground gets tested: bedding, feed crumbs, bugs, shiny things, and yes, droppings.

**2. There's food in it**
Duckling droppings can contain undigested bits of feed, which may attract them.

**3. Boredom**
Ducklings in a small, bare brooder have little else to do.

**4. Nutritional gaps (possible)**
Some keepers suspect ducklings eat droppings more if their diet is lacking. It's another reason to feed a proper duckling or waterfowl starter.

## Why it's worth discouraging

Droppings can spread:
- **Coccidia** and other parasites
- **Bacteria**

A few pecks won't necessarily cause harm, but a **dirty, wet brooder** with droppings everywhere increases the risk of illness.

## How to keep ducklings (and their brooder) cleaner

**Clean often**
Ducklings are messy, and their droppings are wet. Change bedding **frequently**, sometimes daily.

**Separate water from bedding**
Put waterers on a **wire mesh platform** over a tray, or on a separate area that's easy to clean, so bedding stays drier.

**Feed properly**
- Use a **duckling or waterfowl starter**, or a feed your supplier recommends for ducklings
- Ducklings need adequate **niacin** for healthy leg development; many keepers add a niacin source if using chick feed
- Keep feed **fresh and dry**

**Give them things to do**
- Scatter a few **safe greens** (finely chopped) for foraging
- Add a **shallow dish of water** for supervised play (with safe depth)
- Give more **space** as they grow

**Watch for health signs**
Contact a vet if ducklings show:
- **Diarrhea** or bloody droppings
- **Lethargy**, not eating
- **Weakness**, trouble walking
- **Poor growth**

## FAQ

**Why do my ducklings eat their poop?**
Ducklings peck at everything, and droppings may contain undigested feed. Boredom and diet can also play a role.

**Is it bad for ducklings to eat poop?**
It can spread parasites and bacteria, so keep the brooder clean to minimize it.

**How often should I clean a duckling brooder?**
Often. Ducklings are very messy, and many keepers change bedding daily.

**What should ducklings eat?**
A duckling or waterfowl starter feed, with adequate niacin, plus fresh water they can dunk their bills in.
