# Instacart's Clementine: How to Use the New AI Grocery Assistant (With Prompts That Save Money)

Instacart CEO Chris Rogers put it simply: "Every night, millions of families ask the same question: 'What's for dinner?'"

Instacart's answer is **Clementine**, an AI assistant built into the Instacart app. It launched on September 9, 2026, and it's designed to take the most annoying part of grocery shopping, turning "we need food" into an actual list, off your plate.

Here's how to use it and how to get the most out of it.

## What Clementine does

Clementine turns three kinds of input into a ready-to-buy cart:

1. **A conversation.** Tell it what you need in plain words.
2. **A grocery list.** Including a **photo of a handwritten list** or a screenshot of a digital one.
3. **A recipe.** It pulls out the ingredients and adds them.

It can also:
- Create personalized recipes
- Tailor suggestions to dietary needs like gluten-free, vegetarian, nut-free or organic
- Surface deals
- Suggest budget-friendly alternatives

It's available in the Instacart app in the **US and Canada**.

5. Check out as usual.

## The photo-of-a-list trick

This is the feature most people will love. The paper list on the fridge that everyone adds to all week? Snap a photo of it and Clementine turns it into a cart.

Tips for better results:
- Take the photo in good light, straight on.
- If your handwriting is bad (no judgment), add quantities clearly: "milk x2."
- Double-check anything vague. "Cheese" could mean a lot of things.

## 15 prompts that actually help

### Saving money
1. "Build a week of dinners for a family of four for under $100."
2. "Swap anything in my cart for a cheaper store brand when there's one."
3. "What's on sale this week that I could build meals around?"
4. "Plan five lunches using ingredients that overlap, so nothing goes to waste."

### Saving time
5. "Order my usuals."
6. "Easy dinners for two that take under 20 minutes."
7. "A week of kids' lunches that don't need a microwave."
8. "Everything I need for tacos for eight people."

### Dietary needs
9. "High-protein breakfasts, no protein powder."
10. "Vegetarian dinners my meat-eating partner will actually like."
11. "Gluten-free snacks for a school party, nut-free too."
12. "Low-sodium versions of my usual cart."

### Planning ahead
13. "I'm hosting brunch for six on Sunday. Build the cart."
14. "Pantry staples I should always have, based on what I usually buy."
15. "Turn this recipe into a cart, but I already have olive oil, salt and garlic."

Number 15 is worth remembering. Telling it what you already have avoids buying your fifth bottle of olive oil.

## What to watch out for

- **Review quantities.** AI can misread "2" as "12," or pick a family-size pack when you wanted one.
- **Check substitutions.** "Budget-friendly alternative" is great, unless you really needed that specific brand.
- **Allergies need human eyes.** If someone in your home has a serious allergy, check every label yourself. Don't rely on an AI filter alone.
- **Deals can nudge spending.** Suggestions are helpful, but it's still a store. Stick to what you need.

## Is it worth using?

If you already use Instacart, yes. The photo-to-cart feature alone saves real time, and meal-planning prompts beat staring at the fridge. Just keep your eyes on the cart before you pay.

## FAQ

**What is Instacart Clementine?**
An AI shopping assistant in the Instacart app that turns requests, grocery list photos and recipes into shopping carts.

**Is Clementine free?**
It's built into the Instacart app. Instacart hasn't announced a separate price for it. Normal Instacart fees apply to orders.

**Where is Clementine available?**
In the US and Canada.

**Can Clementine read a handwritten grocery list?**
Yes. You can upload a photo of a handwritten list and it will convert it into a cart.
