# Is It Normal for Conures to Nap During the Day? What Healthy Naps Look Like

Conures are loud, busy, playful birds. So when yours tucks its head and dozes off in the middle of the afternoon, you might wonder if something's wrong.

**Short daytime naps are normal for many conures.** Like many parrots, they often rest after meals or during the quieter part of the day. The key is knowing what a *healthy* nap looks like.

*General information. See an avian vet if your bird shows signs of illness.*

## Why conures nap

- **After eating**: a full crop and a relaxed bird often means a snooze
- **Midday lull**: many birds in the wild are most active in the morning and late afternoon, and rest in between
- **Not enough night sleep**: if your conure's nights are too short or noisy, it may catch up during the day
- **Young birds** often sleep more

## Healthy nap vs worrying sleepiness

**Healthy nap:**
- Short, maybe 15–30 minutes or so
- Perched **on one foot**, relaxed
- Feathers **lightly fluffed** but smooth afterward
- Wakes easily and goes back to normal play, chatter and eating

**Worrying signs:**
- **Sleeping much more than usual**
- **Fluffed up for long periods**, looking "puffy"
- Sleeping **on the cage floor** or hunched in a corner
- Sleeping with **both feet** gripping the perch regularly (can be normal occasionally)
- **Eyes half closed** while awake
- **Tail bobbing** with breaths
- Changes in **droppings**, appetite or voice
- Less interested in favorite toys or people

Birds are experts at hiding illness. Increased sleepiness plus any of these signs means **call an avian vet**.

## How much sleep do conures need?

Many avian care sources recommend around **10–12 hours of dark, quiet sleep** at night.

**Tips for better night sleep:**
- **Consistent bedtime** and wake time
- **Dark, quiet room**, away from TV and household activity
- A **breathable cage cover** if it helps your bird settle
- A **dim night light** if your bird is prone to night frights

Good night sleep can also reduce hormonal behavior and cranky moods.

## FAQ

**Do conures take naps during the day?**
Yes, many conures take short naps, especially after eating.

**How long should a conure nap?**
Short naps are typical. Long, frequent sleeping, especially fluffed up, can be a sign of illness.

**How many hours of sleep does a conure need?**
Many care sources recommend about 10–12 hours of dark, quiet sleep at night.

**Why is my conure sleeping so much?**
It could be lack of night sleep, age, or illness. If paired with fluffed feathers, appetite changes or other symptoms, see an avian vet.
