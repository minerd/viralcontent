# Google Assistant Is Gone From Android. Here's What Replaced It (and What Still Works)

If you said "Hey Google" recently and got a different kind of answer, or your old routine stopped working, you're not imagining it.

Google Assistant has been shut down on Android phones. Starting **September 4, 2026**, Google began removing it and replacing it with **Gemini**, its newer AI assistant. The rollout takes a few weeks to reach everyone, so if you still have Assistant today, you probably won't for long.

## What exactly was shut down

Google Assistant is being removed from:

- Android phones and tablets
- Wear OS watches
- Headphones
- Android Auto, when it's running from your phone

And this one is final. Google's wording: once it's removed, "you will no longer be able to use or switch back to Google Assistant on your phone, tablet, or paired devices." The old "Switch to Google Assistant" option inside the Gemini app is going away.

## What still has Google Assistant (for now)

- **Cars with Google built-in** keep Google Assistant beyond September 4.
- **Google Home speakers and displays, and Google TV**, will also move to Gemini eventually, but Google hasn't given a date.

So if your kitchen speaker still sounds like the old Assistant, that's why.

## What's different with Gemini

Gemini is built on a much more capable AI model, so it's better at:
- Longer, more natural conversations
- Follow-up questions
- Writing, summarizing and explaining things
- Understanding what's on your screen

But a lot of people miss Assistant for the boring stuff it did reliably: quick timers, smart home commands, simple routines. Gemini handles those too, but it can feel slower or different, and some old routines don't carry over perfectly.

## How to fix the things that broke

**1. Check your default assistant.**
On most Android phones: **Settings > Apps > Default apps > Digital assistant app**. Make sure Gemini is selected.

**2. Re-check "Hey Google."**
Open the Gemini app, go to settings, and make sure voice activation is on. You may need to retrain your voice.

**3. Rebuild your routines.**
If you had morning or bedtime routines in Assistant, check them in the Google Home app. Some may need to be recreated.

**4. Reconnect smart home devices.**
If "turn off the lights" stopped working, open Google Home and check that your devices are still linked.

**5. Look at Gemini's permissions.**
Gemini can connect to more of your Google apps (Gmail, Calendar, Drive) than Assistant did. Go through what it's allowed to access and turn off what you don't need.

## Why Google did this

Running two assistants is expensive and confusing. Google has put its AI bets on Gemini, which now has more than a billion monthly users, and Assistant was the older technology. Retiring it pushes everyone onto one system.

That makes sense for Google. For users who liked a simple, fast assistant that just set timers without chatting, it's a real loss, and it's fair to say so.

## FAQ

**Is Google Assistant discontinued?**
On Android phones, tablets, Wear OS watches, headphones and Android Auto (from your phone), yes, starting September 4, 2026.

**Can I switch back to Google Assistant?**
No. Once it's removed from your device, you can't switch back.

**Does "Hey Google" still work?**
Yes, it now activates Gemini instead of Google Assistant.

**What about my Nest speaker?**
Google Home speakers and displays will move to Gemini later. Google hasn't announced a date.
