# Why Are My Hognose Snake's Eyes Cloudy? Blue Phase, Retained Eye Caps and Injuries

You glance at your hognose snake and its eyes look **milky, blue or cloudy**. The snake might also look duller and act grumpier than usual.

**Most of the time, it's about to shed.** Here's how to tell, and what to check afterward.

## The "blue phase" before a shed

Snakes don't have eyelids. Instead, each eye is covered by a clear scale called a **spectacle** or **eye cap**. It sheds along with the rest of the skin.

Before a shed, fluid builds up between the old and new skin, making:
- **Eyes cloudy blue or milky**
- **Body color dull**
- The snake more **defensive** (it can't see well) and more likely to **hide**
- Many snakes **refuse food**

This phase usually lasts several days. The eyes then **clear up again**, and the snake sheds within a few days after that.

**What to do:**
- **Handle less** during this time
- **Skip feeding** until after the shed (many keepers do)
- Make sure there's a **humid hide** available

## After the shed: check the eye caps

A good shed comes off in one piece and includes **both eye caps**. Look at the shed skin: you should see two clear, lens-like spots where the eyes were.

**Retained eye caps**
If the eyes still look **cloudy, wrinkled or dull after the shed**, the old eye caps may be stuck.
**Don't** try to pick them off yourself; you can damage the eye.
**Try:** increasing humidity (humid hide), and a short, lukewarm soak. If they're still stuck after the next shed cycle, or you're unsure, see a reptile vet.

## When cloudy eyes aren't about shedding

See a reptile vet if:
- **Only one eye** is cloudy
- There's **swelling**, bulging, or discharge
- The eye looks **injured**, scratched or discolored
- Cloudiness **doesn't clear** and there's no shed
- The snake is **lethargic**, losing weight, or has other signs of illness

## Preventing shed problems

- **Humid hide** with damp moss or paper towel
- Proper **temperature gradient**
- Rough surfaces (rocks, branches) to help with shedding
- Fresh water available

## FAQ

**Why are my hognose snake's eyes blue?**
Usually because it's about to shed. The eyes clear up a few days before the shed.

**Should I feed my hognose when its eyes are cloudy?**
Many keepers wait until after the shed, as snakes often refuse food during this phase.

**What is a retained eye cap?**
When the clear scale over the eye doesn't come off during a shed.

**When should I worry about cloudy eyes in a snake?**
If only one eye is affected, there's swelling or discharge, or cloudiness doesn't clear with a shed.
