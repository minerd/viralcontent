# Is It Normal for Geese to Drool? Water Drips vs Signs of a Problem

Geese are messy eaters and drinkers. They scoop up water, dabble in mud, and shovel food. So a bit of liquid dripping from a goose's bill is usually just leftover water.

But real drooling, especially with other symptoms, can mean something's wrong. Here's how to tell.

*General information. Contact a vet familiar with waterfowl if your goose seems unwell.*

## Normal: water and food drips

Geese:
- **Drink by scooping** water and tilting their heads back
- **Dunk their heads** to clean their bills and nostrils
- **Mix food with water** as they eat

It's normal to see water or a bit of muddy liquid dripping from the bill after eating or drinking. The goose should otherwise be eating, drinking, walking and honking normally.

## When drooling is a concern

Be concerned if you see:
- **Constant drooling** or stringy saliva, not just water drips
- **Gaping** (repeatedly opening the bill) or **stretching the neck**
- **A foul smell** from the mouth
- **Swelling** in the neck or throat area
- **Difficulty swallowing**, dropping food
- **Weakness**, a limp or floppy neck, trouble standing
- **Not eating or drinking**

## Possible causes

**1. Something stuck**
Geese graze on grass and may pick up long grass, string, fishing line, or other objects that can get caught in the mouth or throat.

**2. Mouth or tongue injury**
A cut or puncture from a sharp object.

**3. Infection**
Mouth or throat infections can cause drooling and a bad smell.

**4. Toxins**
Some toxins can cause weakness and neurological signs in waterfowl. A goose with a **floppy neck and weakness** needs urgent veterinary attention.

**5. Heat**
Very hot geese may pant with open bills, which can look like drooling. Provide shade and water.

## What to do

1. **Gently check the mouth** if you can safely restrain the goose (a second person helps). Look for string, grass, fishing line or injuries.
2. **Don't pull** on anything that seems deeply stuck. Fishing line and string can cause serious injury if pulled.
3. **Separate** the goose if it seems unwell.
4. **Contact a vet**, especially if there's weakness, swelling, a foul smell, or the goose isn't eating.

## FAQ

**Why is my goose drooling?**
Often it's just water dripping after drinking or eating. Constant drooling with other symptoms may indicate something stuck, an injury, infection or toxins.

**Is it normal for geese to have water dripping from their beaks?**
Yes, geese are messy drinkers, and dripping water is normal.

**What are signs a goose has something stuck in its throat?**
Gaping, neck stretching, drooling, trouble swallowing and refusing food.

**When should I call a vet for my goose?**
If drooling comes with weakness, swelling, a foul smell, or not eating or drinking.
