# Why Can't I Get Siri AI? 5 Reasons (Including One Nobody Mentions)

You updated to iOS 27. You've seen the videos of the new Siri AI understanding your screen and doing things across apps. And your iPhone still has the same old Siri.

Here are the five reasons that usually explains it, in order of how common they are. The last one catches out a lot of people who bought their phone abroad.

## 1. Your iPhone is too old

Siri AI is part of Apple Intelligence, which needs more powerful hardware. iOS 27 itself runs on iPhone 11 and newer, but **Apple Intelligence requires an iPhone 15 Pro or newer**.

So if you have an iPhone 15 (non-Pro), iPhone 14 or older, you'll get iOS 27's other features but not Siri AI.

**How to check:** Settings > General > About > Model Name.

## 2. You're still on the waitlist

When you first turn on Siri AI, your device may need to join a waitlist and download files before it's ready.

Users on Apple's forums report waits ranging from **a few hours to more than three days**. A tip that comes up repeatedly: leave your iPhone **plugged in and on Wi-Fi overnight** so it can finish downloading. Users also report that once one device on your Apple Account gets access, your other supported devices follow.

## 3. Your language isn't supported yet

At launch, Siri AI works in **English only**. If your device language or Siri language is set to something else, it won't appear.

**How to check:** Settings > Siri > Language, and Settings > General > Language & Region.

## 4. You're in a region where it's not available

Siri AI wasn't available in the **EU or China** at launch. Where you are, and your Apple Account region, can both matter. Rules on AI features vary by country, and Apple rolls out region by region.

## 5. Your iPhone was made for the Chinese market

This is the one almost nobody talks about, and it's the only one on this list you can't fix.

iPhones sold in mainland China are blocked from Apple Intelligence **at the device level**. That means:

- **Changing your Apple Account region won't help**
- **Changing your language won't help**
- **Moving to another country won't help**

On Apple's forums, a user with an iPhone 15 Pro Max bought for China, now living in Africa, found exactly this. A Community member explained that the restriction is tied to the device, because otherwise anyone in China could just change their region settings to get around it. It will only change if Chinese regulators allow Apple Intelligence on those devices.

**How to check if your iPhone is a China model:**
1. Go to **Settings > General > About**.
2. Look at the **Model Number**. Tap it if it shows a number starting with "M" instead.
3. If the model number ends in **CH/A**, it's a mainland China model.

This matters a lot if you're buying a used or imported iPhone. A cheaper phone from overseas might not be a bargain if Siri AI and Apple Intelligence are the reason you're upgrading.

## Quick checklist

| Check | Where |
|---|---|
| iPhone 15 Pro or newer? | Settings > General > About |
| Updated to iOS 27? | Settings > General > Software Update |
| English language? | Settings > Siri > Language |
| Waitlist done? | Leave on Wi-Fi + charger overnight |
| Model number not ending in CH/A? | Settings > General > About |

## FAQ

**Why don't I have Siri AI after updating to iOS 27?**
Your iPhone may be too old (it needs iPhone 15 Pro or newer), you may be on the waitlist, your language or region may not be supported, or your device may be a China-market model.

**How long is the Siri AI waitlist?**
Users report anything from a few hours to more than three days.

**Can I get Siri AI on a China iPhone by changing my region?**
No. The restriction applies to the device itself, so changing your Apple Account region or language doesn't help.

**Is Siri AI available in other languages?**
At launch it supports English only.
