# Is It Normal for Conures to Lose Tail Feathers? Molting vs Damage vs Plucking

You find a long tail feather on the cage floor, then another. Your conure suddenly looks a bit stubby. Should you worry?

**Losing tail feathers can be completely normal**, but it isn't always. Here's how to tell.

*General information. See an avian vet if feathers look abnormal or your bird seems unwell.*

## 1. Molting (normal)

Parrots replace their feathers regularly in a process called **molting**. Tail feathers fall out and grow back.

**Signs it's a normal molt:**
- Feathers fall out **gradually**, not all at once
- You see **pin feathers** (new feathers in waxy sheaths) growing in
- Your conure may be a little **grumpier or itchier** and preen more
- Feathers regrow over the following weeks

**Help during molting:** a nutritious diet, regular misting or baths, and patience. Pin feathers can be sensitive, so handle gently.

## 2. Broken or damaged feathers

**Causes:**
- **Cage too small**, so the tail hits bars or the floor
- **Night frights**: thrashing in the dark can break feathers
- **Rough play** or accidents

**Signs:** feathers **broken or frayed** rather than falling out whole; bent or split tips.

**Fix:** a bigger cage, perches placed so the tail doesn't rub, and a **dim night light** for night frights.

## 3. Broken blood feathers

A growing feather has a blood supply. If it breaks, it can **bleed**.
**What to do:** apply gentle pressure and contact an avian vet, especially if bleeding doesn't stop quickly.

## 4. Plucking or barbering

Some parrots pull or chew their own feathers due to **stress, boredom, hormones, or medical problems**.

**Signs:** chewed or frayed feather edges, bald patches, the bird seen pulling at its feathers.

**Action:** see an avian vet to rule out medical causes, and review enrichment, sleep and routine.

## 5. Illness

Some diseases affect feather growth.

**See a vet if:** new feathers look **abnormal, twisted, discolored, or clubbed**; feathers don't regrow; or there are other signs of illness.

## FAQ

**Is it normal for my conure to lose tail feathers?**
Yes, during a molt feathers fall out and regrow. Broken feathers, plucking or abnormal regrowth are not normal.

**How long does it take conure tail feathers to grow back?**
Usually several weeks, depending on the bird and the molt.

**What is a blood feather?**
A growing feather with a blood supply. If broken, it can bleed and may need veterinary attention.

**Why does my conure keep breaking tail feathers?**
Often a cage that's too small or night frights. Adjust cage size and perch placement, and consider a night light.
