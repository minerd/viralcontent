# Why Does My Uromastyx Open Its Mouth? Gaping, Warnings and Health Signs

Uromastyx (spiny-tailed lizards) are desert lizards that love heat. If you've noticed yours sitting under the basking lamp with its mouth open, you might be wondering if it's okay.

**Usually, yes.** Open-mouth basking is a common way lizards manage their body temperature. But mouth-gaping can also be a warning display or a sign of illness. Here's how to tell.

*General care information. See a reptile vet if you notice any of the health signs below.*

## 1. Thermoregulation (normal)

Uromastyx need a **very hot basking spot** to digest and stay healthy, hotter than many other pet lizards. When they've warmed up enough, they may **open their mouths** to release a bit of heat, similar to panting.

**Signs it's normal:**
- Happens **while basking** or right after
- The lizard is **alert and active**
- **Closes its mouth** when it moves to a cooler area
- No discharge, bubbles or swelling

**Check:** use a **temperature gun or probe thermometer** on the basking surface and in the cool end. Your enclosure needs a clear gradient: very hot basking spot, much cooler cool side, so the lizard can move to regulate.

If your uromastyx is gaping **all the time**, even away from the basking spot, the whole enclosure may be too hot.

## 2. A warning display

Uromastyx may open their mouths, puff up, and whip their spiky tails when they feel threatened.

**Signs:** combined with tail swiping, puffing up, hissing, or backing into a corner. Usually happens when being approached or handled.

**What to do:** move slowly, give space, and build trust gradually.

## 3. Respiratory infection

**Warning signs:**
- **Gaping plus bubbles or mucus** at the nose or mouth
- **Wheezing, clicking or crackling** sounds
- **Labored breathing**
- **Lethargy** and **reduced appetite**

Respiratory infections are often linked to **low temperatures, poor ventilation, or high humidity** (uromastyx are desert animals and need dry conditions).

**Action:** see a reptile vet. Fix husbandry issues too.

## 4. Mouth rot (stomatitis)

**Warning signs:**
- **Redness, swelling, or cheesy-looking material** in the mouth
- Bleeding gums, reluctance to eat
- Holding the mouth slightly open because it hurts

**Action:** vet visit. Mouth infections need treatment.

## Quick guide

| What you see | Likely cause |
|---|---|
| Mouth open while basking, alert | Normal thermoregulation |
| Mouth open all over the enclosure | Enclosure too hot |
| Mouth open + tail whipping + puffing | Warning display |
| Mouth open + bubbles/wheezing | Possible respiratory infection |
| Red, swollen or cheesy mouth | Possible mouth rot |

## FAQ

**Why is my uromastyx basking with its mouth open?**
Usually to regulate its body temperature. It's normal when basking.

**Is mouth gaping a sign of illness in uromastyx?**
It can be if combined with bubbles, wheezing, lethargy or swelling in the mouth.

**How hot should a uromastyx basking spot be?**
Uromastyx need a very hot basking area with a much cooler side. Follow a species-specific care guide and measure with a temperature gun.

**What does mouth rot look like in lizards?**
Redness, swelling, and cheesy or pus-like material in the mouth.
